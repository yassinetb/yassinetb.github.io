        <h1>Privacy Policy — Celeste Assistant</h1>
        <p>Last updated: October 2026</p>
    </div>

<br />

"Celeste Assistant" is a personal automation tool built and operated solely by Yassine Taoudi-Benchekroun, the owner of this website, for his own private use. It is not a public product or service, and no one other than Yassine uses it.

**What it accesses.** With Yassine's own explicit authorization, Celeste Assistant reads Google Calendar (upcoming events, to help prepare daily/weekly summaries) and Google Drive (file listings and contents in folders Yassine has chosen to share with it). It does not request or access any other Google account's data.

**How the data is used.** Data retrieved from Calendar and Drive is used exclusively to help Yassine organize his own schedule and reference his own files. It is processed and stored only on infrastructure Yassine privately owns and controls. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

**Data retention and revocation.** Access tokens are stored securely and used only to make the API calls described above. Yassine can revoke access at any time via [Google Account permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions), which immediately invalidates all stored tokens.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be sent to [ybenchekroun@outlook.com](mailto:ybenchekroun@outlook.com).
